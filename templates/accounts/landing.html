One term, start to finish

Nothing leaves the school until the principal has seen it and approved it.

Teachers enter scores

Each teacher sees only their own classes and subjects. Scores save as they type, even when the network drops. Anyone who prefers a laptop can download the class sheet, fill it offline and upload it back.

The system computes

Totals, grades, subject positions, class averages, highest and lowest scores, overall position and cumulative average. Ties, absences and part-term entrants follow rules you set once.

Class teacher and principal review

The class teacher adds attendance, behaviour ratings and a comment. The principal reads the sheet, comments and approves. Every action is signed and timestamped.

You publish

One click for a class, an arm or the whole school. Parents get an SMS with a link. A published result cannot be quietly edited; corrections create a new version with a visible trail.

Fees, without sending anyone home

The bursar marks a student as owing. That student's result stays locked until the fees are cleared, and the parent sees exactly who to talk to. Clear the payment and access returns the same second.

ReGate tracks the status your bursar sets. It does not run your accounts, hold your money or replace your ledger.

Questions schools ask

Our teachers are not computer people.

Score entry is a list of names with boxes for marks, built for a phone. Training takes about forty minutes and we do it on site. Any teacher who still prefers paper can hand their sheet to the class teacher, who types it in.

What if the network is bad during entry?

Scores save on the phone as they are typed and sync when the connection returns. Nothing is lost when a page drops.

Can we still give parents paper?

Yes. Every result downloads as a PDF you can print, and broadsheets print on A3 or A4.

Our grading is different from other schools.

You set your own assessment components, weights and grade boundaries, separately for nursery, primary and secondary. Nothing is fixed by us.

Who can see our students' data?

Only the staff you give accounts to, and each parent for their own children. We are the processor, your school is the controller, and that is written into the contract. Data is encrypted, every action is logged, and you can export or delete everything on request.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your account becomes read only for 90 days, which is long enough to export every result, broadsheet and transcript. We tell you before anything is deleted.
