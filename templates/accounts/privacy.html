Privacy and data protection

Your school is the controller. ReGate is the processor.

How we handle school data

ReGate processes student, parent and staff data as a processor. The school is the controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

Data is used to compute, publish and retain academic results. Access is role-scoped. Student identifiers are not placed in public URLs except the opaque result verification code.

Results are retained permanently while the school remains a customer, because historical transcripts are a core service. If a school stops paying: read-only access, Excel and PDF export on request, then a 90-day deletion notice.

A breach affecting personal data will be notified to the school so it can notify affected persons as required by law.
